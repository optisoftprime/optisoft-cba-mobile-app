Services We Provide

Our areas of specialization include the following:

Aviation Law

  1. Reviewing various contracts for leasing of aircrafts e.g. wet leases, dry leases, damp leases and maintenance contracts.

  2. Reviewing preparation of standard contracts and terminal procedures, airport maintenance contracts, employment, crew hiring, negotiation with local authorities, aircraft maintenance, parking and facility management for various aviation companies.

  3. Recovery of debt.

Civil & Criminal Litigation

  1. Instituting actions and Defence for clients, corporate and private individuals.

  2. Handling litigation all over Nigeria in all the courts of superior record including the apex court; and all other courts, tribunals, arbitral and mediation proceedings.

  3. Alternative Dispute Resolution

Corporate Matters & Company Secretaries

  1. Registration of companies, business name, Incorporated Trustees and Partnerships.

  2. Carrying out company secretarial services including drafting board resolutions and taking minutes of meetings, company searches, preparing and filing statutory returns and advising on compliance with various regulatory provisions.

  3. Handling Legal advice, reviewing of standard trading contracts, registration of trademark and copyright, shareholding agreements, corporate governance etc.

Family & Probate

  1. Probate and Family matters; Administration of Estates such as Execution of wills, obtaining Letter of Administration etc.

  2. Litigation matters for decree of dissolution; Maintenance and custody of children.

  3. Adoption/ Guardianship

Labour & Employment Law/ Other Specialization

  1. Litigation at the National Industrial Court.

  2. Other area of specialization:- Employment, Labour and industrial actions, oil and gas related issues, land disputes, financial and criminal litigation, commercial transactions, winding up, mergers and acquisitions, dept recovery, contractual relationships.

  3. Company search; land searches, perfection of title, recovery of premises, management.

  4. Expatriate quote: Work/ business permit, immigration, legal documentation.

Legal Drafting and Conveyance

  • Drafting and reviewing agreements including: deeds of assignments, Lease agreements and perfection of title, Contracts of employment, bilateral and tripartite agreements, warehousing agreements, franchising and distributorship agreements, partnership agreements, memorandum of Understanding, property acquisition.

Administrative Matters.

  • Meetings with clients, conducting interviews, preparing updates on status in litigation and initiating arbitration and mediation between parties, preparation of legal opinions and non-legal issues, interfacing with government bodies and parastatals with issues on regulation.

  • International laws and contracts based on international charters and laws.

Membership

  • The firm is an active member of the Nigeria Bar Association, Lagos Branch (Section on Business Law) and the international Bar Association; Arbitration and Mediation and institute of Chartered secretaries (ICSAN); International academy of family lawyers (IAFL).
